Who counts

Zwift lists every rider on the same road, not just your ride — on a busy world that can double your rider count with people who are not with you. Once a rider has been seen in your event they keep counting for the rest of the ride, even if they leave the event or drop out of the list at an intersection, and the ride leader always counts whether or not they are registered. Outside an event nothing is filtered.

Rider history

Counts rides with this ride since you started using the mod — there is no way to recover attendance from before that. On your first ride use Mark all as known in the Riders panel so long-time regulars are not welcomed as new.
Each milestone is announced as one message naming everyone who reached it, so a group who all started together produces a single message rather than one per rider. Keep the gaps wide: a rider hits a milestone about once every gap weeks.
Stops riders who merely pass you on the road from becoming regulars.
0 names everyone, which on a busy week can run to several chat lines.
Off by default: "who's ridden with us before" doesn't mean much for a solo or free ride, so history sits idle until you're in a Zwift event — nothing is saved from one free ride to the next. Turn this on to exercise the feature (e.g. for debugging) without a real event.
Inside an event the ride is identified automatically from Zwift's own event series, so history follows the event rather than you. Only used when "Track rider history outside a Zwift event" above is on.
